How it works

1. The system generates a unique signed QR ID for every student. The vendor prints name, roll number, and QR on each card; students collect the right card by reading their name. 2. Volunteers scan at the gates; every scan is stamped with time, gate, and volunteer. 3. The dashboard counts everything live, including the male/female split, from the exam department roster. 4. Guests get single-use QR passes that cannot be reused.
